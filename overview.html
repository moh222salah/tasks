Handing over is a transaction, not a name change

Most tools swap the assignee and call it done. Here the sender proves what is being passed on, the receiver confirms what they got, and the system records the exact moment responsibility moved.

  1. Draft

    The sender writes the diagnosis, the solution, the results and attaches evidence.

  2. Ready

    Required fields are checked on the server before the handover can be submitted.

  3. Pending acceptance

    Submitting freezes a snapshot and hashes it. A retry with the same request id never creates a second handover.

  4. Under review

    Only the named receiver, or someone they explicitly delegated to, can open the decision.

  5. Accepted

    Owner, assignment and department change in one transaction, and a new ledger row is written.

Reject

A reason and details are mandatory. Ownership does not move. The attempt stays in the history.

Ask for clarification

Questions go back to the sender. Nothing is decided and the owner stays the same.

Revise or hand over again

A new version is created and earlier snapshots are kept. A task can pass through many owners, in order.

Frozen snapshot
What the receiver approved is stored with a SHA-256 hash. Editing the task later cannot change that record, and a database edit of an approved handover is detected when it is read.
One owner at a time
The handover row is locked first, then the task row. A unique key on task and sequence makes a double transfer impossible, even when two people accept at once.
No self-approval
The sender can never accept their own handover, and nobody can forge the sender identity. Acceptance on someone’s behalf needs an explicit, dated, revocable delegation.
Append-only history
Submitted handovers, reviews, history entries and the ownership ledger cannot be edited or deleted. The Administrator account is bound by the same rules in the controller.
Knowledge stays
If a person leaves or changes department, the next owner reads the diagnosis, the decisions and the evidence instead of asking the previous owner.

What the app covers

Employee, Department, Company, Project, Task and File stay the ERPNext originals. The app adds what is missing around them and does not edit Frappe or ERPNext core.

Organization

Departments, teams and memberships are data you manage in ERPNext, not structure fixed in code.

  • Teams with a lead and members; one primary team per person, no overlapping memberships
  • Department profiles with a manager and a deputy
  • Organization chart: departments, teams and members

Tasks after handover

Work continues on the normal ERPNext Task, with a few additions on top.

  • Task updates: append-only notes from the current owner
  • Checklist: a task cannot be completed while a required item is open
  • Quality review by someone who never owned the task

Permissions

Rules are enforced on the server, not by hiding buttons.

  • Twelve roles, from Employee to Read Only Auditor
  • Team leaders see their teams, department managers their departments, receivers what is addressed to them
  • Row scoping through permission query conditions and has_permission

Notifications

Notices that carry responsibility always arrive. Everything else can be muted per user.

  • Hourly reminders: due soon, overdue, escalation to managers; each sent once
  • Daily reminders for tasks due tomorrow or overdue
  • Preferences for reminders and progress updates

Reports and KPIs

Every report is built from live ERPNext data and prints how its numbers are calculated.

  • 14 script reports with filters, export and print
  • Fair rules: cancelled tasks excluded, delays caused by a waiting receiver excused
  • Monthly department snapshots that are frozen once created

Interface

Navy, royal blue and gold, with light and dark modes.

  • Handover Center: inbox, sent, needs my action, drafts
  • Stage bar, vertical timeline and responsibility history on every handover
  • Arabic translation and right-to-left layout

Use a throwaway bench and site. Never try this against a production site first.

bench init --frappe-branch version-16 frappe-bench && cd frappe-bench
bench get-app --branch version-16 erpnext
bench get-app https://github.com/moh222salah/tasks
bench new-site nds-dev.localhost --install-app erpnext
bench --site nds-dev.localhost install-app nds_operations
# development sites only
bench --site nds-dev.localhost set-config allow_tests true
bench --site nds-dev.localhost run-tests --app nds_operations

Production steps — a safe checkpoint, the change, and an immediate rollback — are in the deployment guide.

Safety rules

  • No core editsThe app extends ERPNext and Frappe only through hooks, DocTypes and standard APIs.
  • No secrets in the repositoryNo passwords, tokens or site data are stored here.
  • No unapproved changes to a real siteMigration, restart, update or deploy needs explicit approval, a backup and a rollback plan.
  • Other systems stay untouchedExisting customizations, employee data and transactions are never modified.
