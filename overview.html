    Fair KPI rules

    Performance is not a task count. These rules are covered by tests.

    Waiting is not latenessA task is not counted overdue while its handover waits on the receiver.
    Cancelled work is excludedCancelled tasks leave the measures they would distort.
    Reassigning cannot launder a numberMetrics follow the append-only ledger, not the current owner field.
    Every formula is shownEach indicator says how it is calculated, so a score can be challenged.
    Overload needs contextA heavy load flag needs at least 3 open tasks and more than 1.5 times the team average.
    First-pass acceptanceCounts handovers accepted without a rejection or clarification round.

    Safety rules

    No core editsOnly hooks, DocTypes and standard APIs extend ERPNext and Frappe.
    No secrets in the repositoryNo passwords, tokens or site data are stored here.
    No unapproved change to a real siteMigration, restart, update or deploy needs approval, a backup and a rollback plan.
    Other systems stay untouchedExisting customizations, employee data and transactions are never modified.
